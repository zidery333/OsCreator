## Claude Code extras

Skills live in `.claude/skills/` — type `/save`, `/find`, `/learn`, `/catchup`,
`/wrapup`, `/tidy` or `/make-skill` in the chat. Helpers (`@builder`, `@researcher`,
`@reviewer`) get picked automatically for jobs that deserve their own context.
`.claude/CATALOG.md` lists them all.

These are a convenience. Everything in this folder also works through `./os` alone,
which is what any other AI uses.
